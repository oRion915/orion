# Title

AI Governance and Human Accountability

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-07-22

## Context

AI can accelerate research, drafting, analysis, and routine work, but it can also introduce errors, conceal uncertainty, and weaken accountability when treated as an authority. Orion requires a durable standard for using AI without delegating human responsibility.

## Decision

Treat AI as an assistive collaborator. People remain accountable for goals, decisions, verification, security, privacy, and consequences. AI-assisted work must satisfy the same standards of evidence, review, clarity, and stewardship as all other work.

## Consequences

Contributors must review and validate AI-assisted outputs before relying on them. The use of AI does not excuse opaque reasoning, unverified claims, or diminished ownership. Orion may benefit from AI while preserving human judgment at every consequential boundary.

## Alternatives Considered

- Treat AI output as authoritative when it is efficient or plausible.
- Prohibit all AI assistance regardless of context.
- Allow AI use without explicit accountability expectations.

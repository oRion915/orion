# Title

Project Orion Charter

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-07-22

## Context

Project Orion needs a stable foundation for decisions that will outlast individual features, contributors, and technologies. Without a shared charter, local optimizations can gradually weaken the trust, clarity, and continuity needed for a long-lived system.

## Decision

Adopt the Project Orion constitution as the governing statement of mission, values, and engineering principles. All consequential decisions should be consistent with its commitments to trustworthy, durable, and understandable software.

## Consequences

Contributors have a common standard for evaluating work beyond immediate delivery. Decisions that conflict with the charter require explicit scrutiny and a durable record of their rationale. The charter becomes a living source of continuity while remaining stable in its core commitments.

## Alternatives Considered

- Rely solely on informal team norms.
- Define principles only as individual project needs arise.
- Prioritize delivery goals without an enduring engineering charter.

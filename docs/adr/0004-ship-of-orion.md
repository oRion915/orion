# Title

Ship of Orion Continuity

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-07-22

## Context

Orion is intended to evolve over decades. Its components, contributors, and operating conditions will change, and preservation of every existing part is neither possible nor desirable. The project needs a principle for changing responsibly without losing its essential commitments.

## Decision

Allow Orion to evolve through incremental replacement and renewal while preserving its defining principles, user commitments, and accumulated reasoning. Assess change by whether it improves long-term trustworthiness and maintainability, not by whether it preserves existing parts unchanged.

## Consequences

Modernization is legitimate when it has a clear purpose and protects continuity of user trust. Contributors should favor reversible, well-understood transitions and retain the decision records needed to explain significant change. Stability of values takes precedence over stability of components.

## Alternatives Considered

- Preserve existing structures indefinitely to avoid change.
- Replace broad portions of the project without regard to continuity.
- Treat historical reasoning as unnecessary once a component changes.

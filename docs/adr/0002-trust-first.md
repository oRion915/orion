# Title

Trust-First Engineering

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-07-22

## Context

Users and contributors depend on Orion to behave predictably, safeguard what is entrusted to it, and communicate honestly. Trust is difficult to earn and easy to lose; treating it as a secondary concern creates risks that compound over time.

## Decision

Place user trust ahead of convenience, novelty, and short-term optimization. Evaluate consequential work for its effects on correctness, transparency, privacy, security, reliability, and recoverability.

## Consequences

Some work will require more care, review, or restraint than the fastest path would suggest. Orion will favor clear limitations, accountable behavior, and responsible remediation over appearances of certainty or speed.

## Alternatives Considered

- Treat trust concerns as separate from ordinary engineering decisions.
- Optimize primarily for feature velocity.
- Address reliability and transparency only after user harm occurs.
